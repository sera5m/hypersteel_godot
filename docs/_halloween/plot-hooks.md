# Plot hooks (Halloween branch only)

Non-canon. Do not silently overwrite the main campaign ending.

## Valkarie

There is a scripted death to Valkarie in the plot. There is also a later scripted death after plasma, with wake-up on Athena deep in the plot. That later Athena beat is probably better left alone for the Halloween fork.

The early scripted death on capture can still lead to the after-death cutscene, same as earlier deaths.

Halloween branch idea: you kill / interrupt Valkarie by intersecting reality again inside her plane. Player dies in the early encounter, runs the afterlife, forces a telefrag exit inside her space. Plasma flash and thermal pulse catch her. She goes down as a scripted beat, not a normal damage kill. Player falls, launches, or stands in the wreck.

Robot Valkarie might survive the plasma; low chance. If both survive a mutual detonation, they can both land in the 100 m³ pocket instead of cleanly returning. Then they have to fight out together (or argue the whole time).

Valkarie crashing out verbally before or during the flash is the intended tone.

## Enduring

Sees a plasma return, gets a short explanation, is excited.

He can immediately kill himself and come back with his own plasma explosion once he understands the trick.

In the 100 m³ pocket he is already at the blood bar with a barrel of beer. Hit him and he leaves. Do not hit him and he tag-teams.

## Mini-bosses / elites

Instantly obliterated if a plasma bubble arrives on top of them. No special speech required. Wet slap of blood-mist and a scorched hole.

## Multiplayer / living-world tell

Ryko walking out of a fusion flash is a recognizable tell. Blood-filled cavity plus ozone/iron smell.

Warp-pack no-clip sending people to a Backrooms-like sub-layer was an early multiplayer joke. Keep it optional and small if used. Prefer the black-hole edge-pocket rule if you need a destination that is not infinite-universe slop.
