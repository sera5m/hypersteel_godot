# Edge pockets and the 100 m³ cube

## Cosmology constraint

Avoid Marvel/DC infinite-alt-universe slop. The universe is basically all there is. There can be a large but finite number of universes if needed; do not treat “every death is another Earth” as the model.

Preferred model from later notes:
- Small “edge” pocket bubbles occur only in supermassive black holes inside our universe.
- They are localized geometric failures of this spacetime, not parallel timelines.
- Rare, temporary on cosmic timescales.
- Sealed by event horizon plus the same kind of hypergeometric boundary used for afterlife exits.
- Contents are residual geometry and leaked thought-sludge, which is why they can feel Backrooms-adjacent without being “the Backrooms franchise.”

Warp drives already bend classical 3D. A plasma exit is a more violent version of the same principle. Under high interference (two exits colliding) or extreme curvature, you can glance off into an edge pocket instead of returning cleanly.

Early joke (“the universe is inside a larger Backrooms”) is too silly and is not the baseline. Adjacent pocket is allowed. Infinite slop is not.

## The 100 m³ pocket (Halloween set-piece)

When two high-energy exits collide (player + Valkarie, or equivalent), they can pinch off a single sealed volume on the order of 100 m³.

The pocket exists only while occupants remain. When the last occupant leaves, or the last thing that can still hate you dies, the pocket collapses and stops existing. It does not leave a concrete sphere. It leaves nothing.

Size is the scare. Nowhere to run. Every surface is close. Sound has nowhere to go. Blood layer is deeper because it has nowhere to drain.

### Interior

Angry remainder of unfinished merges: judgment, observation, limited time, “you should not have come back,” plus personal grudges of whoever detonated.

- Walls of inverted obelisk-rat material (matte black). They can breathe inward a few centimeters when you look away.
- Floor like The Shining bar, except not red paint: actual slow blood, ankle-deep, warm, footprints last too long.
- High-density fused afterlife fragments: garden growing out of a burning desk, quiet corridor that is also screaming scales, coral-triangle debris hanging like meat.
- One corner has a bar counter made of the same blood. Enduring can already be there.

### Enduring in the bar

He is not surprised.

Line: “Oh hey. How’s it going? I go here to drink.”

He raises an entire barrel of beer (the only thing in the pocket that is not blood or thought-sludge).

If you hit him, he turns around and leaves. The wall accepts him. He can return the next time a pocket this dense forms. This is his dive bar.

If you do not hit him, he can stay and tag-team.

### Fight

You argue and punch your way out of the most terrifying 100 m³ of space available.

Suggested occupant: the obelisk-king in `bestiary.md`.

Tag-team notes:
- Ryko’s blade phases and smears chip/brain texture, making the thing glitch and show a weak face.
- Valkarie, if she survived transit, is angry enough to treat it as the thing that ruined her moment; she can tank and break line of sight on purpose to force bad reform.
- Enduring drinks, then shoots the exposed face because he has seen this before.

The cube can shrink a little on real hits. Spite is finite. When the king comes apart, the volume folds. Occupants dump back into normal space (sea, orbit, or whatever is funnier for that encounter) with the smeared plasma flash. Pocket is gone.
