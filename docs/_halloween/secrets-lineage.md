# Secrets and gags — lineage

This Halloween special sits in the same family as other Hypersteel secrets/gags, not in the main campaign systems.

## White Knuckle

Taken from, or adjacent to, recent WK updates and regions:

- Periphery universes: other manifolds next to baseline reality. Hypersteel afterlife exits already used this for the exponential boundary and content-swap. Do not turn it into infinite Marvel/DC Earths. Edge pockets inside supermassive black holes only.
- Interlude: Intersection: a short, wrong in-between after taking a hidden route (Hadal Diver skip lands in Intersection). The afterlife and the 100 m³ pocket are interludes, not campaign chapters.
- Habitation skip / Rotten Duct / body cavity / body duct: a shortcut that is worse than the thing it skips. Decaying concrete and blood, fragile holds, things that stop when observed. Afterlife obelisk-rats (4D vermin whose 3D slice looks like a black prism) and the wet maze dodge branch are in this register. Unlock-a-horrible-skip energy, not a power fantasy skip.
- Delta periphery / intersecting baseline: anomalous matter at the seam. Plasma bubble + swapped interiors is the Hypersteel version of “two manifolds forced to touch.”

## Ultrakill

- Secret layers that are real places, not menu jokes only.
- Seasonal / date gags that still have mechanics.
- The April Fools idea already on the Hypersteel repo is inspired by Ultrakill’s dating-sim secret. The trope is played more straight, with a more realistic ending, instead of staying a pure parody.

Halloween should follow that same rule: commit to the bit. The plasma return is funny because it is physically followed through, not because a title card says joke.

## How Halloween should feel as a secret

- Optional or rare (event, then 1-in-50k lottery).
- Hidden route that is smaller, meaner, and conceptually wrong.
- Pays off in the living world (plasma flash, boss branch) the way a WK shortcut still dumps you into an interlude.
- Has its own bestiary. Living-world obelisk turrets stay turrets.
- April Fools = relationship gag played straight. Halloween = death/return gag played straight.
