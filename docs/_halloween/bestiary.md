# Afterlife bestiary (non-canon)

This bestiary is afterlife-only. Do not reuse living-world roster names unless a note says they are the same creature.

Living-world “obelisks” are not these. Living-world obelisks are laser turrets in very tall watchtowers with cameras. See `obelisk-rats.md`.

## Congealants

Reverse weeping-angel behavior.

They only pursue while seen. If out of vision they turn back into energized blood pools.

Pools can be stepped in (slow + damage over time in early notes) or quietly reform behind you.

They make almost no sound except a wet, low-frequency thump when they solidify.

Visibility is relative to observer soul type in later notes:
- Human / catgirl: solid while looked at.
- Robot: error geometry while looked at.
- Ryko: temporary 3D cross-sections of something larger that only fully exists when the blade is pointed at them.

Not personal villains. More like the place reacting to something still trying to cohere or escape.

## Static-and-blood NPCs

Humanoid outlines made of bitmoshed television static and dripping blood/fluid.

They stand in doorways or at corridor ends and watch.

Interact: they dissolve into a short burst of white noise containing about one second of your own voice from an earlier match or death.

## Afterlife obelisk-rats (4D vermin)

See `obelisk-rats.md` for full behavior.

Short version: small four-dimensional creatures. In 4D terms they are the equivalent of a rat. That is how small / low-status they are in their own space. In 3D they present as matte-black obelisk-like cross-sections because that is the slice you can see.

They are not turrets. They are animals of the thought-sludge.

## Obelisk-king (pocket only)

Only described for the 100 m³ interference pocket (`pocket-100m3.md`).

A congealed larger form that has absorbed both the inverted-room behavior and the reverse-weeping-angel behavior.

- Exists fully while at least one person looks at it.
- Becomes a climbing blood pool when no one looks.
- Splits when two people look from different angles and tries to box the viewers.
- Forced into one solid, fightable shape when all three occupants look at once.

Not a living-world boss transplanted here.

## Optional set dressing, not necessarily enemies

- Bisected “astronaut” or equivalent figures floating in a corridor, blood draining the wrong way.
- Floating coral-triangle debris (soul leftovers) that interpenetrate with a bitmosh sound.
- Incomplete Babel shelves and data-shards.
- Burning desks, offering tables with still-warm offerings and no clerks.
- Small persistent fires that never spread on reflective plazas.
