# Exits, telefrag, plasma, respawn

## Ordinary respawn (living world default)

People go back to their body or any body that has their brain in it. In practice this is almost always a printed clone (noted as 99.9999% chance).

This is the normal path. The Halloween afterlife is an exception / overlay / lottery, not a replacement of printing.

## Normal afterlife exit vs violent exit

A soul can leave by sorting enough personal fragments, by the plane getting bored, or by resolving an obelisk-rat encounter with a parry.

When a soul punches back into normal reality, the exit can be violent: a bubble between the afterlife and living space, plasma on the boundary, blood-mist arriving with the soul.

## Telefrag / content swap

If the exit bubble overlaps solid matter in the real world (telefrag):
- Nuclear fusion occurs on the edges of the bubble.
- Interior contents of A and B swap.
- Somewhere in the void there is a solid sphere of whatever was in the real world (concrete, steel, dirt, etc.).
- In the real world there is a bubble of air and blood (afterlife contents).

The boundary layer is an exponential curve between the two 3D universes because of hypergeometry. Partial inspiration: White Knuckle “periphery” universes. Warp drives in-setting already bend classical 3D; this is a more violent higher-order punch.

Layer notes from conversation (keep as written, even if units are messy):
- Layer is ~3 cm⁴ thick but contains ~3 cm³ * 2 of material.
- Bubble on the order of 3 m wide.
- Fusion imagined along the boundary at a rate involving x² / a limit; not a finished equation.

### Energy, then the game handwave

A naive fusion estimate on ~6 cm³ of material (~12 g at ~2 g/cm³) at D-T-like yield is around 4 TJ, on the order of 1 kt TNT. That is too much to simulate and too much for the gag to stay local.

Handwave used for implementation:
- Energy is “smeared” along the hypergeometric interface as the manifolds slide.
- Only a fraction couples into local 3D as heat/plasma.
- Effective yield on the order of 1.2 g fully converted, or otherwise tuned to a bright few-meter plasma sphere, outward heat pulse, vaporize organics at point blank, scorched ring, no city crater.
- Even reflection off a black surface can be written as instantly dangerous at close range, but the engine should not try to be a full radiation sim.

## Who survives the plasma bubble

Only Ryko reliably survives a point-blank plasma bomb of this kind, because she has zero biological components.

A regular human dies before finishing the exit and falls back to ordinary print-respawn (or is just dead in the living world until printed).

Robots are marginal. Robot Valkarie might make it, low chance.

Enduring is treated as able to survive / recreate the trip (see `plot-hooks.md`).

Gag beat:
- Kill Ryko.
- “We did it guys holy shit.”
- Ten minutes later, massive plasma explosion.
- “Hey guys I’m back from the afterlife.”
- “WHAT THE FUCK.”

You come back fine. The method of return is the problem. Came-back-wrong trope inverted: the body is fine; the universe takes the hit.
