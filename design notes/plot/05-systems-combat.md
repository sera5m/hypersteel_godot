# Systems (campaign-facing)

## Fear / rage / taunt
- Taunt button. Language is cheaper than a rocket.
- Enemy tags: scatter / freeze / tunnel / **enrage** (slight power-up, anime/Ultrakill cousin).
- Conscripts scatter. Pros get ugly. Elites ignore cheap taunts unless you deleted their officer.
- Style / threat meter can tick off successful intimidation of high-value targets.
- Do not auto-taunt. Player owns the cheap verb.
- Taunt + melee (live game): middle-finger punch or similar.
- Taunt + melee **after secret-ending flag**: bite (April folder only).

## Ryko enrage
Rare. Campaign once at “SHUT THE FUCK UP.” Same readable flag as enemy rage.

## Objectives
Sticky inner monologue in the multiplayer chat slot. Overwrites when the job changes. Stutters when the why-loop starts. Center-screen only for: headshot, bell, the scream.

## Player = motor / impulse
The player is Ryko’s **motor-control lobe** (or the subconscious that fires movement and cheap verbs). Not a ghost pilot. Not a silent marine.

- You aim, dash, siphon, press Taunt. That is impulse.
- The sticky OBJ line is **executive** — her labeling, her why.
- Early game they agree: intimidate, finish the job.
- Sapience is when executive talks *back* to motor (stuttering OBJ, then the scream, which is not on the taunt button).
- Suit AI death screen is a third voice (hardware), not the player.

Do not narrate this in campaign. It is why the camera is first-person and why she still has a personality you do not puppeteer.

## Motor / executive desync
Impulse can fail when executive vetoes. Scarce.

- Ally fire: shot does not go. Box: `intrusive. discarded.` / `not a target.`
- Scripted withholds (Redline finish): same energy.
- Empty interact: frantic pocket-pat. Screen: `EMPTY`. Repeat = same fidget, impatient.
- Idle: weapon spin / tile-click. Motor bored.
- No autopilot. She blocks and fidgets. She does not play for you.
- **100–200 consecutive misses:** soft aim-assist kicks in. Box: `recalibrating targeting.` She is done with the lobe. Assist drops once you start hitting.

Post-scream vetoes can be `no` (still flat). Earlier = system-flavored.

Grab: Ryko throws almost anything. Enduring can grab her back. Valkarie jets out of a hold. Arsonist cooks you off. Sniper and Redline stick. The bike is hold-or-slip. Full table in `12-melee.md`.

## Heal
Usually armor-strip / gibs / blood. Maw is lore. Kill-to-heal is honest without a cannibal cutscene.

## Limb damage (Ryko)
Per-limb injury exists. It does **not** play like a human wound.

- Redundant + overbuilt. A wrecked arm/leg only **weakens that limb** (dash/punch/aim slightly worse). She keeps moving.
- Kill-to-heal restores limbs. That is the intended loop, not a medkit sit.
- Once a limb is already injured: further hits to that area are **less likely to “count”** unless they are dead-center (smaller hurtbox / glancing off wreckage). Those center hits **penetrate more** (higher chance to punch through the damaged section).
- Read: first injury matters; piling onto the same stump is unreliable unless you aim the hole. Encourages swapping targets / glory-kills to reset rather than dumping a mag into a sparking arm.

Fiction: she is not a meat bag. You are shooting a plant that shrugs until you thread the gap.
