# World notes

## Earth / war
Near-future Sino-Japanese conflict. Geoengineering leftovers, Lost 75 years memory-holed. Fractal drive was a blacksite; copies leak after the campaign. Osmium as nasty vaporizer tech. Hydras are machines (often construction kit stolen into war).

Everyone getting the drive + **Proxima ~1 year superluminal** is post-game pressure. First campaign should not become a Proxima briefing.

## Athena (spare Earth)
Captured ice-giant / failed-giant **core**, not a diet Jupiter. Light terraforming. ~90% Earth mass originally pitched; prefer “compact iron-heavy, feel the steps” or ease mass so taiga hiking is possible. Strong field. Helium shimmer above the air (capture leftover).

Atmosphere breathable, ~31% O2 / 58% N2 / rest mix — keep **some CO2** for a carbon cycle. Fires are mean. Phosphorus scarce, not absent (feedstock bills matter). Soil rust or grey. Sulfides + light metals. Plants yellow/orange; mushroom-tree mix. Poisons if you snack, not if you climb.

Latitudes: north ≈ Antarctica; mid ≈ Canada; equator ≈ Italy. Reduced seasons. Daily weather jumpy (binary with Mars). Oceans stain from not-quite-algae.

Mars is a close companion: still a suit world (~1.8% Earth pressure if using their 3×-Mars-atmo note). Ice, caves, tidal heat. No Phobos/Deimos; junk at the Lagrange. Ceres doubled for flavor.

Pop ~0.5B in interlocking cities (Lost 75 + ~50 years settlement). Titan is the posting everyone hates. Athena is the posting people extend.

Do not smash this planet for aura. Ryko/Valkarie become fire-code insurance after the sting: retired-not-retired. A city gets flattened only if someone tries to recall one of them into the old war.

## Death tech
Nanobots, cortex snapshot, fusion dump. Once sacred (Valkarie headshot UI). On Athena: trays, prefab bodies, public printer queues, resource accounting. Death gets cheap; the bill does not.
