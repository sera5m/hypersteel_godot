# Principles

## Tone
- Operators, not a hero shooter.
- Violent glory-kills. Suit-AI death screen (sacred once).
- Sino-Japanese near-future war.
- World MO: everything **oversize / overbuilt** (cities, kit, bodies, Athena infrastructure).
- Runtime is mostly **unnamed trained soldiers** with fear or menace. Named fights are spikes.
- Humor: Ryko finds being terrifying funny. Not quip-FPS.
- **Commit.** 90s ham stays ham. No self-aware millennial wink. Some people are cartoon-awful; play it straight.
- Kill the death of meaning unless you are deleting a meaning you do not need or it produces the wrong result. Deconstruct to cut fat, not to smirk. If irony shows up as a worldview, **shoot it in the head to Celldweller, loud.**
- Cybergoth is allowed. All-out. Cyber-occult + Metalheart pattern is legal camo in cable spaces. Go and see what happens.
- Loot the rooms. Do not join them. The clothes can be scene / rivethead / Metalheart / Chrome Hearts / cybergoth. The **attitude is not**. Job first. Construction writes numbers. Nothing hidden. God is the audience.
- Named bosses get **stage time** — a way to be the GOAT under pressure.
- **Pyro is acting** (in-universe). Everyone else means it.
- World reads as a **normal tactical shooter / imsim**. Enhanced bodies (Ryko, some cyborgs) do not obey human limits and do not care. The planet is realistic; the player is not.

## Proven kit (Unreal slice + independent review)
Do not reopen as theory.
- Movement: Titanfall.
- Guns: Doom Eternal (swap, distinct, powerful, alt-fire) with tac-FPS / imsim seasoning — not the other way around.
- Anything on screen is canon (Ultrakill / White Knuckle).
- Atmosphere: White Knuckle pressure and industrial mood more than a film pass.

## What is fresh
- Death as a system (foam / cortex / fusion dump). Sacred at Valkarie headshot; cheap on Athena trays.
- Villain works off-screen (server theft during Enduring).
- Protagonist skips the obvious; intimidation is a verb (taunt button).
- Lost 75 undercity is a lived place, not a sewer hat.
- Glory-kills can move plot (Pyro faceplate → Arsonist headset).

## What not to do
- Repeat the sacred death screen.
- Second carrier / second nuke as Act 2.
- Proxima lecture inside the first campaign (stinger only).
- Valkarie ear-racism as a catchphrase after she has data on Ryko.
- Ship the dating sim as campaign canon.
- Make Ryko narrate rooms.
- Cast from a representation spreadsheet. Cast from priors and jobs. Redline exists so Valkarie is not the species.

## Near-miss tropes already dodged
- Open-world checkbox Japan (optional bar on a directed line).
- Catgirl-as-pet (Valkarie is a jackass adult; Redline has a job).
- Boss rush (in-between infantry is the meal; script pile just documented the posters).
