# Characters

## Ryko (player)
Japan JSDF rapid-offense “super” chassis. Merge of human ghosts, Felinis maps, AGI frameworks. She considers herself a person.

**Voice:** not silent; never comments the obvious; flat affect; snide mean streak; enjoys seeming like a menace (funny to her). Casual talk: vaguely interested + impatient. Underground: awkward, straightforward, diagnostic “kindness.”

**Sapience ladder (campaign):**
- Start: almost-sapient. Language + tactics + theory of mind **as a weapon**. Intimidation of high-value targets is a reward loop. Taunt button = cheap fear injector (Ultrakill-style meter justification).
- Objectives live as a **sticky internal line** in the chat slot, not a vanishing quest HUD.
- Pyro/Arsonist: objectives with faces. Mock is instrumental (makes them slip).
- Enduring: donates `combat ≈ identity`. She is still in a fight-reward / data-gather loop; she caches his motto because it scores. Annoyance can start.
- Lost 75 + Redline: **cognitive empathy** (“other people are other selves / what if I was them”), not sympathy. Redline is spared on purpose; inconvenience snipe; she finds the launch funny.
- Valkarie: first emotion **above** fear / attention / reward / interest = annoyance. Enrage. “OH MY GOD SHUT THE FUCK UP.” That is a boundary, not a taunt.
- Valkarie notices immediately (she is sharp). Smug file dies; distressed-crass remains.
- End: focused person, not a robot. Can peer-bond. “Too fun to waste.” “Man….”

**Body (super variant):**
- Mostly non-bio. Humanoid shell so the compressed “oversized human soul” is not dysphoric.
- Five eyes: two normal, two smaller below, one center under fake face/hair. Combat can uncover the lid.
- Face-mouth: talk only. No gut behind it.
- True intake: abdominal maw. Microwave → nanodust. Fridge horror. Usually off-camera.
- In-game heal: strip armor / gibs / blood siphon more often than open hatch. Valkarie may side-eye the siphon without knowing the door.
- B4C + carbon-fiber tiles.
- Chest mini aneutronic fusion (lithium pellets). Auto-shutdown if breached. Combat-only near people (months of leak = cancer risk for others).
- Two primary arms; later two smaller; shoulder pods (grenade / laser).
- Left hand can be pile-driver / explosive pistons (bomb in the hand).
- Ports: electrolysis of water (including from tissue) + plasma for dashes / launches. ~200°C tolerant except chip-brain.
- CNT-cable + motor actuation. Blown apart ≠ dead.

China has not packed plasma like Japan. Valkarie’s aircraft reactor is ~5× the chest unit; she leans microwave / rail / backpack plasma.

## Valkarie
Engineered cat-person (see biology doc). Cyborg, not Ryko-tier. Ace / project manager. Aircraft she can split-attention fly while punching.

**Voice:** sarcastic, crass; outright mean when distressed. Early: unique personal “racism” toward earless humans (“you look incomplete”) — **her defect, not the species**. Drops as she knows Ryko. Distressed-crass stays.

**What actually moves her (meatspace):** not gifts, not taunts, not speeches. Watching Ryko *try not to be intimidating* — effort, awkward, personal. Weirdly endearing. Turns the chassis into a someone. The scream proved a self; holding back personalizes it.

**Arc:** smug and winning → headshot Ryko → she’s back → stressed, mask cracks → scream proves the jumper is a self → station clinch / nuke → Athena “fuck it.”

Not a species mascot. Other cat-people (Redline) are not like this.

**Cast method:** friends + coherent attributes. Priors → behavior. Not backstory-first. Not a representation slot.

**Valkarie influences:** Viper (Titanfall 2) made more interesting; Apex Valkyrie silhouette/name; Project Wingman ace; then original (project manager, ear-defect, distressed-crass).

## Redline
Unaugmented cat-person (they). Geologist. Rocket bike. Rail in the bike. High fight IQ, not superhuman strength. Terrified + landing potshots vs a dying Ryko. Survives. Radio callback. “Building inside you.”

Originally slated as human or robot. Became a cat-person **so Valkarie would not be the species stereotype.** Job first.

## Enduring
French-coded brute / pacing philosopher. Collaboration then fight. Fake death. “I didn’t hear a bell.” Gym-for-the-soul. Philosophy donor.

Priors: time in France + WWII wrestler energy + WWE. Invented, then given a motto.

Spread the voice: stadium = logistics cheese; after siphon = one purity bite; last phase = real tactics. Do not jam the rally into the intro.
Not Handsome Jack: talks to troops and the room, never to a stream.
