basic framework for working from to determine ai stats:


Enemy name:

Reference image:

Breif description :

Base info:

-Enemy class
-mass
-height/width
-walk speed
-sprint speed, if applicable
-can recover from glory kill.
-can enter glory kill state multiple times
-can be picked up
-turn rate (degrees/s)
-tick time
-can jump


Brain info
Intelegence
Fear resistance
Can be enraged
Combat style
How does this ai behave with a team
Can this AI heal itself :
Can be healed by: (ai with [item])
On grabbed by enemy player reaction: try to break hold/try to suicide grenade,etc,etc

Body info:
Damage weaknesses
Damage resistances
Armor tier and data
Health
Weak spots
Strong spots
crit spots

Combat:

Main weapon
Secondary weapon
Melee weapon

Weapon switch Condition (under what circumstances will the ai use a rifle or pistol if it has both? eg: if ai has a sniper and isn't skilled enough to quickscope, it will swap to a pistol if you're close enough to it for 0.6 seconds)

Ordinance (like hand grenades)

Style:
-personality
-how voice sounds
-gender/appearance/remarkable traits
-animation style (what it reflects on personality, if it's not just simple tactical whatnot)


Abilities:
-passive (eg 2% health Regen/s or a high sight radius or passive radar or additional senses)
-active (eg flight,or backpacks)
