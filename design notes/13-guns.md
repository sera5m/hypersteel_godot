# Guns

Right hand. Same packets as melee and plates. Alt is a second fire mode on the same sheet, not a second gun.

Caliber texture: **8mm / Type 88** = 8 mm × 8 cm, ~7.62 class AP. 14 mm ≈ .50 where a gun needs it.

---

## Arc rifle

Charge. Fires a **lightning bolt along the path of a small metal spike**.

Spike = Kinetic. Bolt = Ion / lightning on that line. Charge time writes heat.

---

## Tri-gun

Semi-auto marksman. High rate for semi.

**8mm Type 88**, armor-piercing.

Passive: rapid locks in a **30° aim cone**.

**Alt:** single-target. Three rounds at once into scoped weak points.

---

## Kar8-GL4V

AK in Type 88, **33-round** mag. Bright green.

Underbarrel **acid grenade**, 4 in the tube.

**Alt:** acid grenade, detonates on impact.

> Now rifle melt nanobot and inconvenient man blocking road.

---

## SG-8Th “Volcano”

8-gauge **double-barrel thermite** shotgun.

**Alt:** explosive buckshot.

> Pyro welded two of these together and went full fucking ham.

Thermal primary. Explosive on alt. Recoil as thrust (old movement note).

---

## ISL-92c

Infrared **pulse laser**. Hitscan sniper.

**Alt:** open scope, charge capacitor. High charge: stronger beam, visible trace, **explosive ablation** + burning. Head-front hit: brief blind.

Bounces off reflective (`Mirror`) plates — a little, not a pinball table.

> Based off of anti-drone trophy systems on battleships, this miniature version serves a man-portable role for slaying heavy attack drones and agile combatants.

---

## Heavy plasma repeater

L-STAR cousin. Plasma repeating. **Shock** on the packet.

**Alt:** amp shots → lightning + Ion.

> An all around must have for squads engaging both angry human and mech size entities.

Heat is the magazine.

---

## Smart multi-missile SMG

Ultra-compact. **Gyrojets with shaped charges**.

**Alt:** hold on a target ~3 s to lock. Tracks around walls at modern electronics.

**1/10** rounds fail to explode and instead coat the target in **explosive, glowing powder** (next spark / shot cooks it).

> Light vehicles or armored hulks alike have been felled by earlier versions of this smart ultra compact initially produced by Iran while under blockade in the Kharg wars of 2038, with modern electronics adding the ability to track targets around walls, better long distance reliability, and a far more powerful shaped charge.

---

## EL-44 “Bipolar”

Constant laser beams at **up to 4 targets**, split across whoever is on screen. Priority: closest to screen center.

**Alt:** add lightning, **halve beam count**.

> The bipolar is a recent innovation in energy weapons, allowing an operator who enjoys both variety and smart targeting to engage medium range targets. We definitely did not make it out of factory surplus stock.

---

## DP180

P90-shaped. **Two barrels**, horizontal recoil only (Alternator cousin).

**Special:** small **seeker drone grenade** from the hilt. HUD-marked target.

> The DP180 proved useful in quelling the drone attacks in the machine uprising due to its large magazine, low recoil, and ability to deploy a seeker grenade to destroy mobile fast moving targets marked from your HUD.

---

## 40mm “The Big Iron”

MGL cousin. **Softball** projectile velocity.

**Alt:** bouncing bombs. Each bounce **explodes three times weakly**.

## Ryko campaign board

Not the MP shop. The six-lock smart LMG does **not** go to operators.

### Anti-vehicle

**Miniature railgun**  
Taken off **Redline**. Recovered from a vehicle they found, then cut down. Charge-up **hypercavitating** rail. Kinetic AP that ignores most plates. Charge writes heat.

**Plasma charge cannon**  
**Anti-tank.** ~**2 m** long. Vehicle mount. She unbolted it. Intended mounts are not her problem.  
Main: charge plasma. Short = L-STAR bolt. Hold **~8 s** (Halo plasma-pistol *windup*, not pistol size) = **BFG-class** volume when the bar fills. Heat and stance tax. The gun is still a door.  
**Alt:** seat a **rail rod**, fire a **magnetic spike**. Later plasma wants that target.

**Shockwave rocket**  
RPG-size. Impact **or** coat target/area in **explosive powder** (same family as the SMG 1/10). Coat + plasma = extra plasma into the stuck target. Attracts **plasma and metal** (Ultrakill magnet).

### The rest

**Heavy laser cutter**  
Tool that pauses the kit. Infinite, drains stamina / thrust. Cold bonus, hot penalty. Rebound. Face-blind. Holstered = run crumb.

**Pyro’s super shotgun**  
The Volcano.

**Ryko’s smart rifle**  
LMG. Locks **up to 6**. Tri-gun brain, more targets, high accuracy.  
**Not in MP.**

**Micro-missile battle rifle**  
Missiles **follow the mouse** while fired (Tone cousins). Stick, tick.  
**Alt:** detonate all stuck. Launch along the hit vector.

### How to kill everything (campaign loop)

Not a QTE. Status + packets in order.

1. **Full lock, dump** the smart rifle. Six tracks, empty the box.
2. **Micro-missiles into the ass** (open plate). Stick. Tick.
3. **Magnetic grenade / spike.** Magnet Status. Plasma and metal want that body.
4. **Shockwave rocket** into the coat. Powder or boom.
5. **Detonate missiles.** Launch along the hit vector. Stuck the back + you in front = they come at you.
6. **Punch** on arrival. Mass × Strength × incoming speed.
7. They fly out. **Volcano** on the exit.

Laugh. Doom combo + Tone kit. Door-sized plasma still on her back.

MP can steal pieces (powder, punch, Volcano). Not the six-lock dump.
